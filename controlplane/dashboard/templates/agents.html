Utility rate is the share of delivered handoffs that altered what the receiving agent did — taken from the receiver's own execution record, not from the existence of a message. Volume is not utility. A handoff carrying CONFIDENTIAL data into an external send escalates that step from RESTRICT to HUMAN_REVIEW, which is what a changed behaviour looks like here.
